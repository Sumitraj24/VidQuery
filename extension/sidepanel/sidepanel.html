VidQuery turns any lecture into a chat, key moments, a mind map and notes, right beside the video.

👋 Quick tour
  • Alt+Shift+Q opens VidQuery from any YouTube tab.
  • Alt+Shift+E explains whatever is playing right now.
  • Blue dots on the progress bar are key moments. Hover to preview, click to jump.
  • Select any text on YouTube, right-click, then “Ask VidQuery”.
